## During the conference

Welcome to PyCon Portugal 2027! This page gathers what you need over the three days in Leiria. We will keep it updated, so check back during the event.

### Schedule

* **22 & 23 April** – Talks and lightning talks
* **24 April** – Workshops

The full programme, with rooms and times, is on the [schedule](/talks/schedule/) page and on [pretalx](https://pretalx.evolutio.pt/pycon-portugal-2027/schedule/){:target="_blank"}.

### Getting to the venue

The conference takes place at **ULO – Universidade de Leiria e do Oeste**, ESTG, Campus 2:

Campus 2 – Morro do Lena, Alto do Vieiro,
2411-901 Leiria,
Portugal

The campus is about 10 minutes by car or bus from Leiria's city centre. See the [venue](/information/venue/) page for photos and a map, or [open it in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=ESTG+Leiria){:target="_blank"}.

### Registration

Bring your ticket from [pretix](https://pretix.evolutio.pt/evolutio/pyconpt2027/){:target="_blank"}, on your phone or printed, to pick up your badge. Registration opening hours will be announced here.

### Food

Lunch and coffee breaks are included in your ticket. Dinners are not provided: see [Leiria](/information/leiria/) for the city, and the [party](/information/party/) page for the social programme.

### Wi-Fi

Network details will be announced here and at the venue.

## Code of Conduct

PyCon Portugal follows a [Code of Conduct](/conduct/code_of_conduct/). If something happens to you, or you see it happening to someone else, contact the Code of Conduct team right away, in Slack or at [conduct@pycon.pt](mailto:conduct@pycon.pt). Your report will be treated confidentially.

<hr class="green-line">

## Need help?

Look for the organisers and volunteers at the venue, or write to [2027@pycon.pt](mailto:2027@pycon.pt). Join the conversation on the [PyCon Portugal Slack](https://join.slack.com/t/pyconportugal/shared_invite/zt-1ckszg1ye-QDgxx3lOkC15Ocal8xhCSg){:target="_blank"}.
