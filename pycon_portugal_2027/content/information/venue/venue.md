## Venue

The conference will take place at **ULO – Universidade de Leiria e do Oeste**, in the Escola Superior de Tecnologia e Gestão (ESTG), Campus 2.

## About ULO

<a href="https://www.ulo.pt/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><img src="/static/images/sponsors/ulo.svg" alt="ULO – Universidade de Leiria e do Oeste" class="secondary-logo" width="320" height="52"></a>

The Universidade de Leiria e do Oeste is the public university of the Leiria and West region of Portugal. The ESTG is its school of technology and management, home to the engineering and computer science programmes that make it a natural host for a Python conference.

Learn more at [ulo.pt](https://www.ulo.pt/){:target="_blank"}.
