## Leiria

PyCon Portugal 2027 is heading to Leiria, a lively university city in the centre of Portugal, halfway between Lisbon and Porto.

Leiria sits on the banks of the river Lis under a medieval castle, and is the gateway to some of the country's best known places: the monasteries of Batalha and Alcobaça (UNESCO World Heritage), the sanctuary of Fátima, the giant waves of Nazaré and the Atlantic beaches of São Pedro de Moel, all within a short drive. The city centre is walkable, affordable and full of cafés and restaurants.

## Getting there

* **By plane** – Lisbon airport (LIS) is around 1h30 away by car or bus; Porto airport (OPO) around 2h.
* **By bus** – Frequent express coaches connect Leiria with Lisbon, Porto and Coimbra ([Rede Expressos](https://rede-expressos.pt/){:target="_blank"}, [FlixBus](https://www.flixbus.pt/){:target="_blank"}).
* **By car** – Leiria is on the A1 and A8 motorways, about 130 km north of Lisbon and 180 km south of Porto.
* **By train** – Leiria station is on the Linha do Oeste; connections are limited, so the bus is usually faster.
