## Hospitality

### Where to stay

Leiria is a compact city and most hotels are within a short taxi or bus ride of the venue. We are arranging special conditions for attendees with partner hotels:

* **Eurosol Leiria**, in the city centre.
* **Tryp Leiria**, also in the city centre.

Booking details and discount codes will be published here as soon as they are confirmed. In the meantime, follow us on social media (see the footer) for updates.

## Childcare

Childcare at PyCon Portugal and tech conferences are essential factors that help folks who otherwise may not be able to attend. We want to make sure that parents feel welcome bringing their children to PyCon Portugal and that the availability of childcare is not a barrier to attending.

Childcare application dates for 2027 will be announced here. We look forward to seeing you - and your kids - soon in Leiria!
