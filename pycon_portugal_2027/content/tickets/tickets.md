## Tickets

**Early-bird ticket sales open on 20 November 2026** at [pretix](https://pretix.evolutio.pt/evolutio/pyconpt2027/){:target="_blank"}.

| Ticket | Early-bird price | Who is it for? |
| ------ | ---------------- | -------------- |
| Student | 30€ | Full-time students, or anyone currently without regular income. |
| Individual | 75€ | You pay from your own pocket and are not reimbursed by your employer. |
| Business | 125€ | Your employer pays for the ticket or reimburses your conference expenses. |

Tickets cover the three days of the conference (talks on 22–23 April and workshops on 24 April) and include coffee breaks and lunch. Please don't forget to **add your dietary restrictions when purchasing your ticket!**

If you are from a marginalised or under-represented group and cannot afford a ticket, you should [apply for a grant](/information/grants/). **Please consider making an extra donation** to help us offer grants.

If you wish to amend your order or if you have a ticket claim (for instance through a sponsorship or as a speaker), please get in touch at [2027@pycon.pt](mailto:2027@pycon.pt).
